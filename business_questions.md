# Business questions and intended decisions

## 01. Which acquisition channels generate the most signups?
**Why it matters:** Channel mix; assess volume and dependence on any one source.

## 02. What percentage of new users activate within seven days?
**Why it matters:** Activation is an early indicator of onboarding effectiveness.

## 03. At which stage does the learning funnel lose the most users?
**Why it matters:** Prioritise UX or content fixes at the largest drop-off.

## 04. Which acquisition channels generate the highest booked revenue per acquired user?
**Why it matters:** Avoid optimising solely for cheap signups; compare downstream value.

## 05. How does month-one retention vary by signup cohort?
**Why it matters:** Identify whether retention is improving or deteriorating over time.

## 06. Which subscription plan has the highest cancellation rate and how quickly do users cancel?
**Why it matters:** Inform pricing, packaging and early lifecycle interventions.

## 07. Which support issue categories take longest to resolve?
**Why it matters:** Find operational friction that may be damaging user experience.

## 08. Which campaigns have the lowest cost per signup?
**Why it matters:** Compare acquisition efficiency, then interpret alongside user value.
